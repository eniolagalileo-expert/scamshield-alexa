# Common scams and how to spot them

**The golden rule:** real companies and government agencies never ask you to pay with gift cards, crypto or wire transfers, and never ask for a code that was texted to you.

## Fake delivery texts
"Your package is on hold, pay a small fee." Postal services don't text you links to pay fees. Check tracking only on the carrier's own app or website.

## Bank and account alerts
"Your account is locked, verify now." Don't use the link. Open your bank's app or call the number on the back of your card. Banks never ask you to move money to a "safe account."

## Family emergencies
"Hi Mum, this is my new number" or "Grandma, I'm in trouble, don't tell anyone." Call your family member on the number you already have before sending anything.

## Job offers that are too good
High pay, no experience, interviews only over Telegram or WhatsApp, or a check to buy equipment. Real employers never ask you to pay or to deposit their check.

## Prizes, grants and loans
If you have to pay a fee to receive money, it's a scam.

## Tech support
Pop-ups or calls saying your computer is infected. Microsoft and Apple don't call you, and you should never give anyone remote access.

## Government threats
Calls or texts threatening arrest over taxes or fines. Agencies contact you by mail first and never demand gift cards.

## Romance and investment
Someone you've never met in person asks for money or promises guaranteed crypto profits. Guaranteed returns don't exist.

**If you already paid or shared details:** contact your bank right away, change your passwords, and report it.
