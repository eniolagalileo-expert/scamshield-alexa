What ScamShield is

ScamShield is a free, open-source tool that helps you judge whether a message, link, phone number or call may be a scam. It gives guidance, not a guarantee. It can miss scams and can flag genuine messages as suspicious.

Using the answers

  • Always contact a company or agency directly, using a phone number, app or website you already trust, before paying, sharing a code, or giving personal details.
  • ScamShield does not give legal, financial or security advice. If you have already paid or shared details, contact your bank right away and report it to the authorities listed with each result.
  • Don't use ScamShield to test, write or improve scams, or to overload the service. Automated heavy use may be rate-limited or blocked.
